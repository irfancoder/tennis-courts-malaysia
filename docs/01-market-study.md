# Market Study — Tennis Court Directory, Malaysia (tennis.irfanismail.com)

_Research date: 7 Oct 2026. Labels: **[V]** = verified against a cited source; **[I]** = inference/estimate. Google Trends values are relative indices (0–100) and are only comparable within the same comparison set._

---

## 1. Executive summary

1. **Demand for finding and booking tennis courts in Malaysia is real and growing fast.** Google Trends interest in "tennis court" in Malaysia rose **~4.2× from 2021 to 2026** (yearly index 16 → 68) [V]. The fastest-rising related queries are all about booking or access: "book tennis court" +170%, "tennis court booking" +110%, "tennis court rental" +90%, "indoor tennis court" +50%, "tennis courts near me" +170% (12 mo) [V].
2. **Nobody serves the "how do I actually get on this court?" question.** Booking apps (Courtsite, AFA) list only partner venues and are badminton/futsal-heavy. Council portals are Malay-first, split by council and often ask for a MyKad. Global Tennis Network's ~88 KL listings date from 2011–2023, with no prices or hours and some misclassified courts. Nothing consistently covers hotel, club, condo or university courts [V].
3. **The travelling-player audience exists but is a niche.**
   - Malaysia received 42.2M visitors in 2025 and has ~120k expats [V].
   - Malaysians made 290M domestic trips in 2025 [V]. This is an under-appreciated "travelling within Malaysia" segment.
   - Traveller questions are real but long-tail: TripAdvisor threads ask about non-guest hotel court access in Langkawi, KL courts and KL tennis shops [V].
   - Worldwide, searches for "tennis holiday" and "tennis camp" have grown ~5× since 2021 [V].
   - **Most Malaysian search demand is local intent** ("near me", "booking") [I]. So **build for the traveller, which also serves locals**: if a page works for someone with no local knowledge, it works for everyone.
4. **Absolute search volumes are unverified.** Malaysian searches are almost all in English ("gelanggang tenis" is ~0 relative to "tennis court") [V]. City-level queries are tiny [V]. My estimate is low thousands of searches a month nationally for the head term [I]; **validate this in Google Keyword Planner before investing heavily.**
5. **Adjacent opportunities, ranked by evidence:**
   1. indoor/covered courts (strong, inside tennis)
   2. padel (small but ~doubling every year)
   3. tennis-travel content and hotel-court guides (strong globally)
   4. pickleball (large but **down ~40% in 2026**, with existing incumbents)
   5. hitting partners, coaches, stringing and tournament calendar (weak or no measurable search demand)

## 2. Demand evidence

### 2.1 Tennis search interest in Malaysia (Google Trends, MY, Oct 2021–Oct 2026) [V]

| Year | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 YTD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| "tennis court" index | 16.2 | 23.4 | 24.9 | 40.7 | 61.2 | 67.9 |

- Related head-term queries: "tennis court booking" (100), "indoor tennis court" (43), "pickleball court" (42), "book tennis court" (35), "kelana jaya tennis court" (31), "tennis court rental" (13).
- "tennis near me" rose from ~0–6 (2021–25) to 21.6 in 2026.
- "tennis coach", "tennis lesson" and "tennis class" score near 0 relative to "tennis court". **Courts are the demand.**
- Seasonality: highest May–Sep (Jul ≈ 52), lowest Nov–Mar (≈ 33–37). This is partly skewed by the growth trend.
- City modifiers (KL, Penang, JB, KK, Langkawi) score 0–1.9. **City pages will each get modest search traffic**; venue pages plus "booking" and "indoor" intent pages matter more.
- Searches are in English; Malay-language variants are negligible.

### 2.2 Tennis vs pickleball vs padel vs badminton (court searches, MY) [V]

| Year | tennis court | pickleball court | padel court | badminton court |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2023 | 3.8 | 0 | 0 | 50 |
| 2024 | 6.2 | 6.3 | 0.1 | 57.2 |
| 2025 | 9.2 | **24.2** | 0.5 | 51.8 |
| 2026 YTD | 10.2 | 14.8 | 0.7 | 41.7 |

- **Pickleball** peaked in Jul–Aug 2025 and is down ~39% in 2026. It is still larger than tennis.
  - More than 400 venues and 2,500 courts reported [V, press].
  - Councils are converting tennis courts to pickleball [V].
  - JB-for-Singaporeans guides already exist (picklejohor.com, thepicklebase.com) [V].
- **Padel** is ~1/15 the size of tennis-court searches but roughly doubling each year.
  - Rising queries: "sukan padel" (Malay for "padel sport") +850%, "padel asian games 2026" (Breakout).
  - At least 34 venues [V].
  - Playtomic is active in Malaysia for padel only [V].
- **Badminton** is the biggest court search but is declining and fully served by Courtsite and similar apps.

### 2.3 Traveller audience [V unless noted]

- **Inbound visitors:** 42.2M in 2025, including same-day Singaporeans. H1 2026: 21.1M (+2.5%). The Visit Malaysia campaign was extended to the end of 2027.
- **Source markets:** Singapore 8.3M (Jan–May 2025); Indonesia, China, Thailand and India follow. Markets likely to play tennis (UK, AU, JP, KR, Europe) are each ~1–1.3% of arrivals, ≈ 1–2M visitors a year combined [I].
- **Residents:** ~120k expats on employment passes; DE Rantau digital-nomad passes only ~2k (negligible).
- **Domestic trips:** 290M domestic visitor trips in 2025. Malaysians playing in another state (KL → Penang/Langkawi/KK) are a real segment [I].
- **Global travel-tennis searches** (worldwide Trends, 2021 → 2026): "tennis holiday" 6 → 35, "tennis camp" 10 → 59, "tennis bali" 0.3 → 14.5, "tennis bangkok" 0 → 12.6.
- **Tennis events:** no ATP/WTA event since 2017/18. ITF juniors (J200 KL, Apr 2026) and ITF M15 (KL, Penang) are in the LTAM calendar. Event-driven travel is small.
- **What traveller forum posts ask** (TripAdvisor, Expat.com, Lowyat):
  - Can non-guests use the hotel or resort court?
  - What does it cost, and how do I book?
  - What surface is it (grass or hard)?
  - Where can I buy or restring a racquet?
  - How do I find someone to hit with?
  - Are the "free" public courts still there?

## 3. Supply side: what exists and how access works

### 3.1 Venue categories [V with examples; prices need re-verification before publishing]

| Category | Examples | How you get in | Typical RM/hr |
|---|---|---|---|
| Federal/national | National Tennis Centre, Jalan Duta (13 outdoor + 3 indoor) | Courtsite / counter / WhatsApp academy; under facelift since Sep 2025 | 6–24 (dated) |
| Local council | DBKL Titiwangsa, Bangsar; MBPJ Kelana Jaya; MBPP Padang Polo | Council portal (KLSPEF/Tempah KL, MyPJ, Ezy MBSJ, MBPP eTempahan), counter, or walk-in | 5–20 |
| Private club | KLGCC, Royal Lake Club, Royal Selangor Club, Bukit Kiara, Kelab Rahman Putra | A member signs you in / reciprocal membership / membership | 20–55 (guest) |
| Hotel/resort | Mandarin Oriental KL (non-guests RM120 first hr), Eastin PJ, Rasa Sayang, Langkawi resorts | Usually guests; some rent to the public; policies rarely published | 35–120 |
| University | UM (UMPoint), USM (usmbiz), UKM, UiTM, Sunway | Booking window for outsiders sometimes; rules unclear | unknown |
| Condominium | D'Mayang, Kiaramas Cendana, many others | Residents and their invited guests only | n/a |
| Commercial/academy/indoor | Rekt-It (rooftop, covered), RALLE simulators, Glenmarie, SS Tennis KK | Book online (Rezerv etc.) or WhatsApp | 35–140 |

- Night rates are typically 1.3–2× the day rate.
- Surfaces are mostly acrylic hard court; artificial grass is common at clubs; clay is virtually absent.
- Covered/indoor courts are scarce and valued, which matches the "indoor tennis court" search trend.
- Size: an estimated **300–500 venues anyone can book, or 1,000+ including condo and school courts** [I]. GTN lists ~120 venues nationally.
- Concentration: Klang Valley ≫ Johor > Penang > Sabah > Sarawak > Perak > Melaka > Pahang [I].

### 3.2 Problems travellers hit [V/I]
- **Phone:** OTP sign-ups need a +60 number; many venues book only by WhatsApp or phone.
- **Language and ID:** council portals are Malay-first, often ask for a MyKad, and it's unknown whether a passport works.
- **Payment:** FPX online banking (Malaysian banks only), Touch 'n Go / DuitNow QR, cash at counters. Foreign cards may fail.
- **Membership:** clubs need a member to sign you in; reciprocal members usually can't bring guests.
- **Stale information:** pickleball conversions, renovations (Jalan Duta), closures, years-old prices.
- **Weather:** afternoon storms and monsoons (east coast and Sabah, Nov–Mar). Rain/refund policies are rarely stated.
- **Gear:** racquet and ball rental is rare outside academies and simulators.

## 4. Competitive landscape

| Player | What it is | Tennis strength | Gap we exploit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Courtsite (386 centres, ~800k users) | Booking marketplace + venue software | Has Jalan Duta | Partner venues only; no club/hotel/condo; no access guidance |
| AFA Superapp | Booking + community, ~95 venues | Small | Same as Courtsite |
| Council portals (DBKL, MBPJ, MBSJ, MBSA, PPJ, MBPP) | Official booking | Cheapest courts | Malay-first, split by council, no English how-to → **content gap with ranking potential** |
| Playtomic | Padel marketplace | None in MY | — |
| Reclub (143k MY users) | Social meetup tool | Active tennis meets | Complement, not competitor: link out |
| Global Tennis Network | Global directory + community | ~88 KL listings | Stale (2011–23), no prices/hours/booking method, misclassified |
| TennisCall | Global directory | Unverified (blocked fetching) | Likely similar to GTN |
| tennis.my "Playgrounds" | Editorial court reviews | Handful of high-quality pages | Small coverage; not a directory; closest direct competitor |
| Listicles (MyWeekendPlan, TimeOut) | SEO articles | A few venues | "Price not specified"; dated |
| hotels-with-tennis.com, TripAdvisor filters | Hotel finders | "Has court" flag | No non-guest access, price or surface |

**Global models worth learning from:**
- **Pickleheads:** crowdsourced court data plus programmatic SEO pages, 9k → 335k organic clicks a month in 14 months; later became a federation's official finder.
- **Rally:** GPS-verified crowd reports with two-person confirmation before publishing.
- **LTA / USTA court finders:** federation-run booking networks; monetised through software fees.

### Gaps we can own
1. **Access status per court** (who can play, what you need, how to book, how to pay). No one publishes this.
2. **English "how to book" guides for each council system**, including foreigner-specific steps.
3. **Hotel and resort court access for non-guests**, with prices.
4. **Venues no booking app lists:** clubs, universities, condos, hotels.
5. **Verified freshness:** "last verified" date, price day/night, status (open / renovating / converted).
6. **Strong coverage outside the Klang Valley** (Penang, Langkawi, JB, KK, Ipoh), which is thin everywhere today.

## 5. Adjacent use cases — verdicts

| Use case | Evidence | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Indoor/covered court finder** | "indoor tennis court" top-3 related query, +50% rising; rain and heat | **Build in MVP** (filter + landing page) |
| **"How to book" intent pages** | "book tennis court" +170%, "booking" +110%, "rental" +90% | **Build in MVP** (core) |
| **Tennis-travel destination guides** (Langkawi, Penang, KL) + hotel courts | Global "tennis holiday/camp" ~5×; TripAdvisor threads | **Phase 2**, natural affiliate income (hotels) |
| **Padel courts** | Small base, ~2× a year, Asian Games 2026 inclusion; incumbent only Playtomic | **Phase 2 candidate**: re-check Trends in 6 months |
| **Pickleball courts** | Large but −39% in 2026; incumbents (picklejohor, OpenCourt365, Reclub) | **Don't lead.** Only flag tennis courts shared with or converted to pickleball |
| **JB courts for Singaporeans** | Strong for pickleball/padel; **no evidence for tennis** | Cover JB as a city; no dedicated campaign |
| **Hitting partners / social tennis** | Search ≈ 0; forum anecdotes; GTN groups, Reclub exist | **Don't build.** Link to Reclub meets and community groups per venue |
| **Coach directory** | "tennis coach/lesson" ≈ 0 relative; existing directories | **Defer.** List academies as venues only |
| **Stringing / shops** | ≈ 0 search; one TripAdvisor thread | **Defer.** Possible "traveller essentials" mention |
| **Tournament calendar** | Weak; LTAM already publishes one | **Don't build.** Link to LTAM |
| **Condos with tennis courts (renters/buyers)** | Indirect: Mudah, Flatfy, Nuroa and waa2 publish "condo for rent … tennis court" search pages; none describe court quality. Trends not checked | **Phase 2 candidate.** Low cost because condo listings are already in scope |
| "tennis court builders" (Breakout) | Business-to-business query | Out of scope |

## 6. Risks and unknowns
- **Absolute volume unknown.** Trends shows growth, not size. → The owner decided not to run Keyword Planner. Volume will be measured after launch through Google Search Console (PRD D1).
- **Data goes stale** (prices, conversions, renovations). This is the main reason competitors fail. → Verification workflow is a core requirement, not an extra.
- **Liability and privacy** for condo and club courts. → Never imply someone can get in without permission; no resident contact details; no "renting" of condo courts.
- **One-person maintenance.** → Scope launch geography tightly; use crowdsourced corrections.
- **Pickleball conversions** shrink public tennis supply. → "Converted" status and change history.
