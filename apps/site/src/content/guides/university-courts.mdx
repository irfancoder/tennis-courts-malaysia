University courts are mainly for students and staff, but some universities open bookings to the public at set times.

## Examples

- **Universiti Malaya (UM):** courts C and D can be booked through UMPoint, on weekdays from 5pm to 8pm. Public pricing is not clear yet. See [UM's sports centre](https://pusatsukan.um.edu.my/facility).
- **Universiti Sains Malaysia (USM), Penang:** facility bookings are sold through [USMBiz](https://usmbiz.usm.my/product/booking/).

We are confirming public access at other universities, including UKM, UPM, UiTM, Sunway and Taylor's.
