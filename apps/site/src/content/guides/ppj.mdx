Putrajaya Corporation (Perbadanan Putrajaya, PPJ) lists its sports facilities and rates online. Its published rates are among the lowest in the Klang Valley, at roughly RM4 to RM8 per hour.

## Where to book

- **PPJ sports booking:** [spt.ppj.gov.my/sport](https://spt.ppj.gov.my/sport)
