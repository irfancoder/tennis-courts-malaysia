Many condominiums in Malaysia have their own tennis courts. They are private: only residents and the guests they invite can play.

## If a resident invites you

- **The resident books the court** through building management or the residents' app. Guests cannot book directly.
- **Register at the guardhouse** when you arrive. Have your IC or passport ready, and give the unit number and resident's name you are visiting.
- **Follow the house rules.** Courts often have booking limits, guest limits and quiet hours.

## Please don't

- Try to enter or book without a resident.
- Contact building management to ask for court access as an outsider.

We list condominium courts so residents, invited guests and people choosing a home know what courts are there. We never publish residents' or management contact details.
