The Ministry of Youth and Sports (Kementerian Belia dan Sukan, KBS) runs youth and sports complexes (*Kompleks Belia dan Sukan*, often shortened to *Kombes*) around the country. Their courts are booked through the KBS STF portal.

## Where to book

- **KBS STF portal:** [stf.kbs.gov.my](https://stf.kbs.gov.my/ks_user/login.php)

## How it works

At [Kombes Paroi](/courts/negeri-sembilan/seremban/kombes-paroi/) in Seremban, the process is:

1. Register and book your slot on the STF portal.
2. Go to the complex counter and pay in cash before you play.

Bring cash. Card and e-wallet payments were not accepted at the counter when we last checked.
