Subang Jaya City Council (Majlis Bandaraya Subang Jaya, MBSJ) takes court bookings through its Ezy MBSJ portal.

## Where to book

- **Ezy MBSJ:** [ezy2.mbsj.gov.my/facility/booking](https://ezy2.mbsj.gov.my/facility/booking)

## Before you book

- Bookings may need approval first. MBSJ asks for payment within 7 days of approval.
- We are confirming tennis rates and whether visitors without a MyKad can register.
