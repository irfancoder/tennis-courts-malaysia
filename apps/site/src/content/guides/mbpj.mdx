Petaling Jaya City Council (Majlis Bandaraya Petaling Jaya, MBPJ) runs public courts including the Kelana Jaya and SS3 courts, which are among the most searched-for courts in the Klang Valley.

## Where to book

- **MyPJ Tempahan app:** [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.mbpj.etempahan)
- MBPJ's booking terms (in Malay): [syarat tempahan gelanggang (PDF)](https://www.mbpj.gov.my/sites/default/files/syarat_tempahan_gelanggang_dan_padang_mbpj.pdf)

## What it costs

Older reports put Kelana Jaya at around RM10 for two hours. We are confirming current rates.

## Before you book

- Payment is online by card or online banking.
- Walk-in play may be possible when courts are free. Ask at the counter.
