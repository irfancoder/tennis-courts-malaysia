The [Tun Razak National Tennis Centre](/courts/kuala-lumpur/kuala-lumpur/tun-razak-national-tennis-centre/) at Jalan Duta has 13 outdoor courts and 3 indoor courts. It is run by Perbadanan Stadium Malaysia, and public recreational bookings are open to everyone.

## Ways to book

- **Online:** through [Courtsite](/guides/courtsite/), linked from Perbadanan Stadium Malaysia's own venue page.
- **By phone:** the venue office on +60 3-6206 1237.
- **At the counter:** during office hours.
- **Coaching:** the Duta International Tennis Academy is based here and takes bookings over WhatsApp.

## Good to know

Perbadanan Stadium Malaysia announced a facelift for the centre in September 2025, including court repairs and new floodlights. Some courts may be closed while work is under way, so check availability before you travel.
