[Courtsite](https://www.courtsite.my/) is Malaysia's largest sports booking marketplace, with several hundred venues across many sports. It is in English, shows real-time availability, and is the easiest booking option for most visitors.

## What you can book

Courtsite lists venues that partner with it. For tennis that includes the [Tun Razak National Tennis Centre](/courts/kuala-lumpur/kuala-lumpur/tun-razak-national-tennis-centre/) at Jalan Duta. Most council, club, hotel and condominium courts are not on Courtsite, which is why this site exists.

## Booking steps

1. Find the venue on Courtsite and choose tennis.
2. Pick a date, court and time slot.
3. Sign up or log in, then pay online.
4. Show your booking at the venue if asked.
