Kuala Lumpur City Hall (Dewan Bandaraya Kuala Lumpur, DBKL) runs some of the cheapest public courts in the city, including [Titiwangsa](/courts/kuala-lumpur/kuala-lumpur/titiwangsa-tennis-courts/), Bangsar, Wangsa Maju and Astaka.

## Where to book

- **KLSPEF portal:** [appsys.dbkl.gov.my/klspef](https://appsys.dbkl.gov.my/klspef/)
- **Tempah KL app:** [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=my.gov.tempahkl.app)

Both are mainly in Malay. Look for *Tempahan* (booking) and *Gelanggang* (court).

## What it costs

Reported rates are around RM10 per hour in the day (8am to 7pm) and RM20 per hour at night (8pm to midnight). Some courts have no lights. We are confirming current rates.

## Before you book

- Registration may ask for a MyKad (Malaysian IC) number. We are checking whether a passport works for visitors.
- Payment is online, usually by FPX (Malaysian online banking).
- Some venues ask to see your booking receipt at the counter, so keep it on your phone.
